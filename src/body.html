    Hand tracking
    • Two hands pull apart to open, push together to close
    • Pinch and drag orbit the robot
    • Quick pinch click a part or a button
    • Fist, then open open the suit
    • Flick an open palm switch robot

    Start the hand-tracking script first

    pip install mediapipe opencv-python aiohttp
python3 jarvis.py

    Needs Python 3.9 or newer and a webcam. The script opens this viewer at http://localhost:8765. Camera frames stay in the script; only hand points reach the page.

    This view needs WebGL.

    Turn on hardware acceleration in your browser settings, or open this page in a current Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
